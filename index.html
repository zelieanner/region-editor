Region editor

Pencil draws: Polygon = tap the corners, then tap the first corner, double-tap, or "Close shape". Lasso = draw around an area. Replace all: every pixel of one region (e.g. all blotch) becomes the selected paint. Brush = paint; − / + size; Straight = straight lines (a tap draws straight on from the last stroke). Fingers: one finger moves the photo, two fingers pinch to zoom and twist to rotate (it snaps upright when close; Fit straightens it). "Inside outline only": head / body / stripe / blotch only land inside the white outline, so you can go roughly around the snake's outside edge. Kinked marks the whole photo (saved at once, exported with your edits). Your work is saved on this iPad as you go ("in progress"). Fixed = done, next photo. Looks right = nothing to fix. Export edits sends everything you did to the Mac (AirDrop), where import_ipad_edits() reads it. Eye and Glare (shiny highlights on the scales) are left out of all colors; the head counts as body. With a keyboard: 1 eye, 2-4 body / stripe / blotch, 5 glare, E not snake, 6 model's guess, P / L / B tools, [ ] brush size, Z undo, Shift = straight brush line, Enter closes a polygon, S fixed, G looks right, K skip, arrows previous / next.
